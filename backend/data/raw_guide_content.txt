1. IIT Delhi Summer Internship 2026
Program:
Faculty-mentored, hands-on research internship under IIT Delhi departments.
Eligibility:
- UG/PG students from recognised institutions
- Typically pre-final year or students available full-time during summer
Stipend:
- No fixed central stipend.
Stipend varies by department, project, or funding source.
(Some projects are paid, some unpaid; amounts may range roughly Rs 5k-15k/month based on past cycles.)
Duration & Mode:
~6-8 weeks (May-June/July) | On-campus
How to Apply:
- Visit IIT Delhi official website
- Search: "IIT Delhi Summer Internship / SRIP 2026"
- Register -> upload CV & mark sheets
- Select department/project preferences
- Submit before the official deadline
Expected Timeline (based on past cycles):
- Notification: Feb-Mar 2026
- Applications: Mar-Apr 2026
- Selection: Apr-May 2026
- Internship: May-Jul 2026

- Hostel & mess charges are extra

How to Apply:
https://surge.iitk.ac.in/
- Online application only
- Upload documents & preferences
- Deadline: 22 Feb 2026

4. IIT Roorkee SPARK Internship 2026
Program:
SPARK - Structured Program for Academic Research & Knowledge
Eligibility:
- UG/PG students (B.Tech / B.E / BS-MS / B.Arch / M.Sc / MA)
- Minimum 2 semesters completed
- At least 1 semester remaining after summer
Typical CGPA Cut-offs:
- IITs/IISc: > 7.5
- NITs/IISER/NISER/IIEST/UM-DAE CBS: > 8.0
- Other institutes: > 8.5
Stipend & Benefits:
- Rs 2,500 per week (for institute-funded internships)
- Certificate on completion
- Access to campus facilities
Duration & Mode:
~6-8 weeks (May-July) | On-campus
How to Apply:
https://spark.iitr.ac.in/
- Register & login
- Upload marksheet, photo, CV
- 500-word research statement
- Select faculty/projects
Timeline (typical):
- Applications open: Mid-Feb 2026
- Deadline: March 2026
- Internship: May-July 2026

Branch-wise search checklist
Computer Science / IT / AI / Data: software engineering, AI/ML, data science, cybersecurity, cloud, systems, research labs, NIC, C-DAC and university faculty projects.
ECE / EEE / VLSI / Embedded: semiconductor firms, telecom, electronics R&D, power utilities, automation firms, DRDO/ISRO project calls and IIT/IISc labs.
Mechanical / Automobile / Production / Mechatronics: OEMs, manufacturing plants, robotics, energy, industrial R&D and mechanical research institutes.
Civil / Architecture / Planning / Geomatics: infrastructure firms, structural labs, CBRI, water resources, transport, GIS/remote sensing and architecture studios.
Chemical / Petroleum / Polymer / Materials / Metallurgy / Mining: process industries, refineries, steel, polymers, CSIR labs, materials labs and geology/mining research units.
Biotechnology / Biomedical / Pharmacy / Food / Agriculture: biotech and pharma R&D, hospitals/health technology firms, ICMR/ICAR institutes, food labs and life-science research centres.
Environmental / Energy / Renewable / Ocean / Earth Sciences: environmental labs, renewable energy companies, NIH, NIOT, IIRS, GSI, CSIR-NEERI and faculty projects.
Science, Mathematics, Economics, Design and interdisciplinary fields: IISERs, TIFR, IISc, IIT research projects, design labs, UX/product internships and incubated startups.
